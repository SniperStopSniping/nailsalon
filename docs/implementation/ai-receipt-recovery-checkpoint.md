Verified receipts restore completed status and settle measured usage once; domain replay returns the saved result without another model call. Manual revisions cannot manufacture an AI receipt by reusing its operation ID. Assistant contact repairs now carry their actual server-side writer origin; direct manual controls keep their owner origin.
