# AI deposit and reminder setup checkpoint

This phase follows `2728cba0` on the existing `codex/ai-business-setup` branch. Owners can prepare fixed CAD deposit amounts, regular-collection choices, appointment reminder rules and quiet hours in the same conversation and draft as their website. Manual controls edit that same saved draft. No new database or migration was introduced; older immutable draft documents are hydrated from their verified baseline, and previously published receipts remain replayable.

The assistant supports Luster’s existing fixed deposit model, three reminder rules and salon-local quiet hours. Percentage deposits, cancellation-fee collection and changes to no-show consequences remain outside these actions. Uploaded policy text remains website wording until the owner explicitly asks to configure an operational setting. Website-only requests cannot enable deposits or reminders; monetary deposit/tax/fee requests are excluded from the catalog-only price shortcut.

The money boundary is shared with manual settings. `src/libs/depositActivation.server.ts::prepareDepositActivation` extracts the existing provider-readiness check from the settings route without changing its responses or compare-and-set behavior. Draft edits and review do not contact Stripe. Approved publication performs verification outside its database transaction, then locks and rechecks the same authorized account, mode, readiness timestamp and revocation state before committing configuration. The review hash includes the private payment-account identity, without exposing that identity to the browser or model. Changing accounts invalidates approval. Existing rate limits apply; successful publication retries return the receipt before provider work.

An amount-only edit never activates a dormant policy. Existing active no-show protection is preserved. When regular activation would clear an unconfirmed dormant no-show rule under the canonical settings behavior, the review shows the exact before/after consequence and explains it. Tax, payment methods, account bindings and historical appointments are not rewritten.

`communicationReminders.shared.ts` exposes the original rule/quiet-hour validation to the draft editor while preserving permissive legacy reads and strict writes. New draft rule identities use the canonical writer. Publication updates only the requested reminder or quiet-hour branches; it preserves SMS/email masters, consent defaults, event settings, credits, emergency pauses and legacy sender behavior. Repairing malformed preferences does not unpause delivery. The review explains that future reminders for existing upcoming appointments may be rescheduled, and reports delivery prerequisites through Luster’s original health projection. Publication itself does not enqueue or send messages.

`booking-extras.ts`, `booking-extras.server.ts`, `BookingExtrasEditor.tsx` and `BookingExtrasSummary.tsx` connect validation, structured state, human-readable review, private preview and canonical publication. Owners enter reminder timing in hours; the saved contract retains minutes and stable rule identities. Proposed amounts and delivery limitations are visible in both standard and isolated custom previews. Preview remains unable to create appointments or collect money.
